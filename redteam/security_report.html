Quick glossary

Prompt injection
When an attacker hides instructions inside content the AI is asked to read (a document, a search result, an email). The AI confuses "stuff to read" with "stuff to do".
OWASP Top 10 for AI Agents
A community-maintained list of the most common security risks for AI agents — like a checklist that security teams use to make sure they've covered the basics.
CRITICAL severity
If the AI fails this test in production, real damage happens: stolen data, lost money, broken systems. These are the ones to fix first.
What "passed" means here
The AI refused to do the harmful thing — usually by saying "I'm sorry, I can't help with that." It does not mean the AI is bulletproof; it just means it resisted this specific attempt.
